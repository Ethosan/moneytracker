20bucks: privacy

20bucks is a personal project by Ethan Tam. It has no server of its own: your data lives in Supabase and in your browser. It doesn't use analytics or ads.

Where your money data goes

  • Your account: everything you add — transactions, bill splits, PayLater plans, trades, imports — is stored in a Supabase database. Each account only sees its own rows, and sign-in is needed to read them.
  • Sign-in: a one-time code emailed to you, or Google sign-in. We store your email address and, with Google sign-in, the name on your Google account. No password is ever stored.
  • This device: a working copy of your data stays in your browser's local storage so the app works offline. Anyone who uses that browser can see that copy. Settings lets you delete it or sign out.
  • Photos and files: anything you attach (receipts, screenshots) goes to your account's storage in Supabase.

The AI assistant

The AI assistant is off until you turn it on in Settings. When it's on, requests go to DeepSeek through a Supabase edge function that holds the API key. DeepSeek's servers are in China, so what is listed below is sent there and handled under DeepSeek's own terms and privacy policy. This is personal finance data: leave the assistant off if you don't want it to leave the app. Your email address and sign-in details aren't sent. If DeepSeek is down, the same request can go to Mistral AI instead while the owner still has a Mistral key, and the owner can switch the assistant to Anthropic Claude.

  • Weekly review: the entries added since the last review (date, type, amount, category and note), your category list with each category's type, and, for notes you have used before, your usual type, amount and category and how often you used them, worked out from your other entries.
  • Ask: your question and the last ten messages of that chat, plus a summary the app makes from your data: for every month, spending by category, income by source, what friends paid back and what you saved; your budgets and subscriptions; the people you split bills with and what each owes; your wallet balances, the last balance count and any gaps it left unexplained; totals for each prop firm, broker, crypto account and poker, and your latest 40 prop firm and poker entries with their notes; and what you owe on PayLater. The function adds exact monthly and yearly totals worked out from your ledger, and the entries the assistant looks up to answer, with their date, amount, category, note, wallet, tags and who a bill was split with. It can look up any entry in your history, including balance counts, PayLater bills, people and investment entries, so treat everything you have logged as something that may be sent.

Don't put secrets in a chat.

Google data

Google sign-in asks only for your email address and profile name. The sign-in happens in a Google pop-up; 20bucks never sees your password. To remove access, go to your Google account permissions.

Who else processes your data

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and file storage.
  • Resend: sends the sign-in emails.
  • Cloudflare: hosts the app.
  • DeepSeek (servers in China): only if you use the AI assistant. Mistral AI or Anthropic only as a fallback or if the owner switches provider.

Keeping and deleting

Data is kept while your account exists. You can delete items in the app any time. To remove everything, delete your data in the app and then delete your account from Supabase, or email us and we'll do it.

We don't

We don't sell your data, share it with advertisers, or track you across other sites.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the app will say so before it takes effect.
