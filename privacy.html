20bucks: privacy

20bucks is a personal project by Ethan Tam. It has no server of its own: your data lives in Supabase and in your browser. It doesn't use analytics or ads.

Where your money data goes

  • Your account: everything you add — transactions, bill splits, PayLater plans, trades, imports — is stored in a Supabase database. Each account only sees its own rows, and sign-in is needed to read them.
  • Sign-in: a one-time code emailed to you, or Google sign-in. We store your email address and, with Google sign-in, the name on your Google account. No password is ever stored.
  • This device: a working copy of your data stays in your browser's local storage so the app works offline. Anyone who uses that browser can see that copy. Settings lets you delete it or sign out.
  • Photos and files: anything you attach (receipts, screenshots) goes to your account's storage in Supabase.

The AI assistant

The AI assistant is off until you turn it on in Settings. When it's on, requests go to an AI model (Mistral AI by default, or Anthropic Claude) through a Supabase edge function that holds the API key; your email address isn't sent.

  • Weekly review: the entries added since the last review (date, type, amount, category and note), your category list, and your usual amount, type and category for those notes, worked out from your other entries.
  • Ask: your question, the last ten messages of that conversation, and your whole history so the answer has context: every transaction with its notes, wallets and tags, the names of people you split bills with and what they owe, investment, prop firm and poker entries, PayLater bills, balance counts, subscriptions, budgets and wallet balances.

Don't put secrets in a chat.

Google data

Google sign-in asks only for your email address and profile name. The sign-in happens in a Google pop-up; 20bucks never sees your password. To remove access, go to your Google account permissions.

Who else processes your data

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and file storage.
  • Resend: sends the sign-in emails.
  • Cloudflare: hosts the app.
  • Mistral / Anthropic: only if you use the AI assistant.

Keeping and deleting

Data is kept while your account exists. You can delete items in the app any time. To remove everything, delete your data in the app and then delete your account from Supabase, or email us and we'll do it.

We don't

We don't sell your data, share it with advertisers, or track you across other sites.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the app will say so before it takes effect.
