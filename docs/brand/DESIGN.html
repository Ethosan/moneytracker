One bold moment per screen.

20bucks already has a look: a cool grey ground, white cards with 22px corners, one blue accent, green and red for money, Geist with tabular figures, and one dark hero card that holds the month's number. This guide names that look so the app, the GitLab repo and its card on ethantam.dev read as the same thing. It invents nothing.

Every colour below is a token in app.css at v1.4.1. The app follows the system theme unless you pick one, so both themes are listed. The stylesheet's own radius rule: cards 22px, controls 14px, chips and buttons a full pill.

The mark

The icon is a stack of three coins seen edge-on: two outlined rings in ink and a filled one on top in rose, on a warm off-white ground. Inside the app there is no logo; the name is set as text.

What it is

Three ellipses stacked on a square, full-bleed ground. The bottom two are open rings in ink (#12161B); the top coin is filled rose (#E3337A, the app's --rose, the colour it gives the shared-spending tag). The ground is #F6F4EF. The rose is the only colour in it.

Where it sits

icons/icon-192.png is the favicon, a manifest icon and the icon on both home-screen shortcuts (Add a transaction, Insights). icon-512.png is the manifest's any icon and icon-512-maskable.png its maskable one. The splash, the lock screen and the sign-in card show the name as text, never the icon.

Don't
Don't turn the coin blue or green. Blue is the accent for buttons and the active tab; green and red mean money.
Don't stretch it. The coins are ellipses already; squashing them again reads as a mistake.
Don't crop the any icon into a circle. Use icon-512-maskable where the platform masks.
20 Bucks
Don't split or capitalise the name. It is one lowercase word: 20bucks.

Colour

Blue does the work; it is the accent for buttons, links, focus, the active tab and the add button. Green and red are about money, and the app reads spending the way a budget does: spending up is red, spending down is green. Rose is kept for one meaning, spending done together.

Voice

From PRODUCT.md and the copy already in the app.

Plain and calmFull sentences, written to the owner: Nothing changes until you approve. Your data syncs to your own private account. No password needed.
Trust over featuresA wrong total is worse than a missing feature. Anything uncertain is shown for approval, never applied quietly.
Readable over a shoulderFriends see the screen. Clear at a glance, no clutter, no jargon.
Private by defaultThe data stays in the owner's own Supabase. Real transactions never appear in public material, screenshots or commits.
The wordswallet, PayLater, bill, instalment, payback, balance count, people, venue, prop firm, tag, subcategory.

Loose ends

Things this guide found and did not change.

  • PRODUCT.md names the product Ethan's Ledger (short name Ledger); the app, page title and manifest say 20bucks.
  • The feedback button is green: data-accent="#16a34a" on the widget script in index.html. That is not a 20bucks colour (--accent is #2A55F5, --gain is #08784F).
  • The icon's ground (#F6F4EF) and ink (#12161B) are warmer than the app's tokens; only the rose matches.
  • No og: tags, so a shared link unfurls without an image.
  • The icons exist only as PNGs; there is no vector source in the repo.
